# Privacy Policy

**Carters Care Platform**  
**Effective Date:** April 2025  
**Last Updated:** April 2025

---

## 1. Introduction

Carters Care ("we", "us", "our") operates a care management platform for aged care and disability service providers in Australia. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information About Service Providers (Our Customers)
- Business name and ABN
- Contact details (name, email, phone)
- Billing information
- Staff and administrator accounts

### 2.2 Information About Care Recipients (End Users)
- Full name and preferred name
- Date of birth
- Contact details
- NDIS participant number (where applicable)
- Aged care recipient ID (where applicable)
- Emergency contact information
- Care plans and support needs
- Incident reports
- Progress notes and case notes

### 2.3 Information About Support Workers
- Full name and contact details
- Employment information
- Qualifications and certifications
- Working With Children Check status
- NDIS Worker Screening status
- Timesheets and shift records

### 2.4 Technical Information
- IP addresses
- Browser type and device information
- Usage analytics
- Error logs

---

## 3. How We Collect Information

We collect personal information:
- Directly from you when you register or use the platform
- From your employer (service provider) when they add you to the system
- From care recipients or their representatives
- Automatically through cookies and analytics tools

---

## 4. Purpose of Collection

We collect and use personal information to:
- Provide and operate the platform
- Enable care coordination and service delivery
- Manage staff rostering and timesheets
- Generate invoices and reports
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
- Improve our services
- Respond to support requests

---

## 5. Disclosure of Information

We may disclose personal information to:
- **Service providers** using our platform (their own staff and client data)
- **Government bodies** when required by law (NDIS Commission, Aged Care Quality Commission)
- **Third-party service providers** who assist our operations:
  - Supabase (database and authentication)
  - Vercel (web hosting)
  - Cloudflare (security and CDN)

We do NOT:
- Sell personal information
- Use personal information for marketing without consent
- Transfer personal information outside Australia without appropriate safeguards

---

## 6. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures including:
- Encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- Multi-factor authentication for administrative access
- Role-based access controls
- Regular security updates and patching
- Audit logging of data access
- Secure backup procedures

---

## 7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for:
- **Active accounts:** Duration of service plus 7 years
- **Care records:** 7 years from last service date
- **Incident reports:** 7 years minimum
- **Audit logs:** 7 years minimum
- **Technical logs:** 12 months

After retention periods expire, data is securely deleted or de-identified.

---

## 8. Your Rights

Under the Privacy Act, you have the right to:
- **Access** your personal information
- **Correct** inaccurate information
- **Request deletion** where legally permissible
- **Withdraw consent** for optional processing
- **Lodge a complaint** about our handling of your information

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@carterscare.com.au

---

## 9. Cookies and Analytics

We use essential cookies for:
- Authentication and session management
- Security purposes

We may use analytics tools to understand platform usage. These tools collect anonymized data only.

---

## 10. Children's Privacy

Our platform may contain information about minors receiving care services. This information is collected and managed by authorized service providers in accordance with their legal obligations and duty of care.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be communicated via the platform or email. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
